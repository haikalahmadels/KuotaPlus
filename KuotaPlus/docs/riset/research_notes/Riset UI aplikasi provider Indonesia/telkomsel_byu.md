# Telkomsel group self-care apps: MyTelkomsel and by.U (UI, brand, mascot, motion, marketing, sentiment)

Research date: 2026-10-30. Scope: MyTelkomsel (Telkomsel PraBayar / Halo / IndiHome / Orbit, plus MyTelkomsel Lite) and by.U. Store pages show dates without a year for recent versions; years marked "(year inferred)" are inferred from version ordering. No in-app screenshots were viewed directly (the App Store pages served placeholder images, and the Play Store pages could not be fetched), so screen-level layout detail is thin and comes from official text.

## Home screen layout, navigation, and where buy/top-up live

### Takeaway
MyTelkomsel's home is a single dashboard showing pulsa (credit), quota info, and masa aktif (validity) at a glance. Under it sit personalized offers ("For You", Flash Sale) and lifestyle mini-apps. Navigation moved from a hamburger menu to a 5-tab bottom bar back in v4.2, and the home page was rebuilt again in 9.2 (April) and given a "fresh look" in 9.3. by.U is narrower: one app for the whole lifecycle (buy SIM, pick number, build package), with uPlan, uTainment, and Updates pages.

### Cited Findings
- MyTelkomsel App Store description: "Dashboard" lets users check credit, info, and validity on one screen. Users can switch between Telkomsel, IndiHome, and Orbit accounts in one app — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/mytelkomsel/id651412430)
- Package-buying features: personalized data-plan recommendations, repurchase of favorite plans, roaming plans. Promotions include a "For You" section, Flash Sale, and packages for streaming/gaming/productivity — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/mytelkomsel/id651412430)
- Version history (App Store): 9.0.1 (dated 02/08/2025) added "Telkomsel and IndiHome navigation", PIN login, Telkomsel Wallet by GoPay, and "For You" cashback. 9.1.0 added a pop-up top-up feature, quota-low reminders, and a more intuitive billing page. 9.2.0/9.2.1 (Apr) added a **new homepage**, a Finance menu with QRIS scanning, and real-time promos. 9.3.0/9.3.1 (Aug/Sep) added a **"fresh look"**, personalized recommendations, and "Siscamling" protection against suspicious numbers. 9.4.0 (12 Oct) added offers picked for you with each transaction, gifting, subscription management, and transaction-history scrolling — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/mytelkomsel/id651412430)
- Bottom navigation (official FAQ, MyTelkomsel v4.2, older, roughly 2019): the hamburger menu on the left was replaced by a bottom menu with **5 tabs: Home, Rewards, Shop, Explore, Profile**. Telkomsel described the UI as "cleaner and more minimalist" — [Telkomsel FAQ About MyTelkomsel 4.2](https://www.telkomsel.com/en/support/faq/about-mytelkomsel-42)
- The super app relaunch was announced 17 July 2024. Telkomsel described its look as a "fresh, clean, futuristic" redesign with a modern minimalist layout for easier navigation, AI-based search, and login with a Telkomsel, IndiHome, or Orbit number — [Telkomsel press release, 18 Jul 2024](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/mytelkomsel-hadir-sebagai-super-app-yang-memberikan-kemudahan-transaksi-dan)
- MyIndiHome users were told to migrate to MyTelkomsel before January 2025 — [Bisnis.com, 17 Jul 2024](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20240717/101/1783134/superapp-mytelkomsel-hadir-pengguna-myindihome-wajib-migrasi-sebelum-januari-2025)
- MyTelkomsel Lite (Feb 2020, older): under 10 MB, simple design, easier login; core features are check pulsa, check quota, and buy packages/pulsa, aimed at old Android phones and low storage — [Telkomsel news, 4 Feb 2020](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/aplikasi-mytelkomsel-hadirkan-versi-terbaru-untuk-tingkatkan-user-experience). Telkomsel also has a "MyTelkomsel Basic" light-app article — [Telkomsel Jelajah](https://www.telkomsel.com/jelajah/jelajah-lifestyle/download-mytelkomsel-basic-aplikasi-ringan-dengan-fitur-lengkap)
- by.U: everything is digital (SIM purchase, number selection, package setup), and users build their own package (quota plus add-on "topping") in the app — [Selular.id, 19 Oct 2025](https://selular.id/2025/10/by-u-tembus-10-juta-pengguna-kuasai-pasar-digital-operator/)
- by.U app pages named in release notes: **Updates, uPlan, uTainment** (UI/UX changes in 1.59.0, 20 Nov 2024), plus **Ustream, Ustamp, Mini Games, Mixtape** (1.60.0, 13 Jan 2025) — [App Store by.U](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/by-u-id/id1483475992)
- by.U has a "Rincian Pemakaian" (usage detail) view showing remaining quota/topping and validity, plus transaction and usage history — [Telkomsel Jelajah](https://www.telkomsel.com/jelajah/jelajah-lifestyle/cara-cek-kuota-byu-tanpa-aplikasi-ini-mudah-banget)

### Inferences
- MyTelkomsel's home hierarchy is likely: account switcher (Telkomsel/IndiHome/Orbit), then a balance/quota/validity card, then personalized offers ("For You", Flash Sale), then lifestyle/mini-apps. This is inferred from the store description order, not from a screenshot.
- The "pop-up top-up" (9.1.0) and quota-low reminders show that top-up is surfaced contextually as well as from the home card.
- Whether the 5-tab bar (Home/Rewards/Shop/Explore/Profile) survived the 2024 and 2025–26 home redesigns is unverified. The 9.0.1 note about "Telkomsel and IndiHome navigation" suggests the tab structure changed.

## Package catalog, cards, badges, checkout

### Takeaway
MyTelkomsel sells through personalized recommendations, favorites/repurchase, Flash Sale, and use-case categories (streaming, gaming, productivity), and it covers Prabayar, Halo, IndiHome, Orbit, and Telkomsel One in one app. by.U's signature is a "build your own package" model (quota plus topping, choosable validity) with transparent pricing and named tiers.

### Cited Findings
- MyTelkomsel buys Prabayar, Halo, IndiHome, Orbit, and Telkomsel One packages in one app. It also has "auto gift" (automatically buy a package for someone else every month). Payment options include bank transfer, e-wallet, and credit card — [Telkomsel press release 2024](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/mytelkomsel-hadir-sebagai-super-app-yang-memberikan-kemudahan-transaksi-dan)
- "One Click Buy" for pulsa, internet, SMS, entertainment, and roaming packages, plus LinkAja/GoPay payment (Feb 2020, older) — [Telkomsel news 2020](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/aplikasi-mytelkomsel-hadirkan-versi-terbaru-untuk-tingkatkan-user-experience)
- Telkomsel Wallet by GoPay is used for payments and for storing refund balance. LinkAja and SmartPay linking came in 8.x — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/mytelkomsel/id651412430)
- by.U package families: "Jajan" (short validity of 1–14 days), "Super Kaget" (14 days, larger quota), "Kaget" (largest, up to 30 days). "Atur Kuota Sendiri" lets users set quota and validity themselves — [search summary of Telkomsel/Selular articles](https://selular.id/2025/09/paket-malam-by-u-tidak-ada-ini-alternatifnya/) (the package names came from a search-result summary; treat as needing confirmation)
- by.U advertises "no hidden fees", clear upfront terms, and "kuota utuh berlaku 24 jam". It has no dedicated night package — [Selular.id, Oct 2025](https://selular.id/2025/10/by-u-tembus-10-juta-pengguna-kuasai-pasar-digital-operator/)
- by.U 2020 pricing ranged from IDR 4,000 / 1 GB / 1 day to IDR 50,000 / 10 GB / 30 days, with social-media "topping" add-ons (older) — [Telkomsel news, 4 Mar 2020](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/konsisten-hadirkan-ragam-inovasi-fitur-terbaru-byu-perkuat-pengalaman-gaya-hidup)
- by.U merchant vouchers became stackable in 1.64.x (Apr–Jun, likely 2026) — [App Store by.U](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/by-u-id/id1483475992)
- by.U app store description: "choose quota packages based on needs and budget"; quota is usable anywhere, any time, on 3G/4G — [App Store by.U](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/by-u-id/id1483475992)

### Inferences
- MyTelkomsel uses recommendation-first merchandising ("For You", "picked for you with each transaction" in 9.4), so the full catalog is a secondary path. Combined with the user complaints below about inconsistent prices, this is the "hidden cheapest package" risk.
- by.U's composable package (base quota plus toppings plus validity) is a useful pattern for a student app: show a price total that updates live as the user builds the package.

### Gaps
- Badge labels ("Terlaris", "Rekomendasi", "Best Deal"), card anatomy, filter chips, and checkout step count: not found in any text source. They need screenshot verification.

## Typography, brand identity, color, iconography, dark mode

### Takeaway
Telkomsel's 2021 rebrand introduced a custom typeface, **Telkomsel Batik Sans**, plus a batik-inspired "portal" symbol and sonic branding. Official hex codes were not found. by.U has a separate youth brand identity, but no font or color specification was found.

### Cited Findings
- New identity launched June 2021. The typeface Telkomsel Batik Sans was drawn by Deni Anggara (Degarism Studio, Bandung) and inspired by batik curves. The rebrand was led by The VCCP Partnership, with SomeOne handling strategy and identity — [Gizmologi](https://gizmologi.id/news/telco/logo-baru-telkomsel/); [Campaign Asia](https://campaignasia.com/article/telkomsel-unifies-50-identities-in-brand-relaunch/470443); [SomeOne case study](https://someoneinlondon.com/projects/telkomsel)
- The rebrand unified roughly 50 identities. simPATI, Kartu As, and LOOP were merged into Telkomsel PraBayar (monolithic brand) — [Campaign Asia](https://campaignasia.com/article/telkomsel-unifies-50-identities-in-brand-relaunch/470443); [Telkomsel press release](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/telkomsel-perkenalkan-identitas-baru-sebagai-simbol-perubahan-untuk-bukasemuapeluang)
- A third-party guideline site describes the palette as red, turmeric (yellow), and navy, but states that it uses Poppins as a stand-in font — [madegooddesigns](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=118828) (unofficial; low confidence)
- The MyTelkomsel UI has been described as minimalist, mostly white with dark-blue type (from a search summary of a secondary source; low confidence) — [UPN Jatim thesis](https://repository.upnjatim.ac.id/54010/1/BAB%201.pdf)
- by.U's brand image is "quite different from Telkomsel's", with the tagline "Semuanya Semaunya" and the slogan "by youth, for youth" — [Telkom Univ. repository](https://openlibrary.telkomuniversity.ac.id/pustaka/files/167582/bab1/pengaruh-iklan-youtube-iya-juga-ya-terhadap-brand-awareness-produk-by-u.pdf); [Selular.id 2025](https://selular.id/2025/10/by-u-tembus-10-juta-pengguna-kuasai-pasar-digital-operator/)

## Animations, micro-interactions, gamification

### Takeaway
Gamification is strong in both apps. MyTelkomsel has Daily Login, Wheel of POIN, Unlock Code events, Game Hub with daily check-ins and the "Stamp Berhadiah" game, and running challenges with prizes. by.U has Ustamp (stamps), Mini Games hub, uCoin loyalty levels, and anniversary/birthday quota gifts. No sources describe splash screens, skeletons, or success animations.

### Cited Findings
- Rewards tab: Telkomsel POIN, Unlock Code, and Events including **Daily Login and Wheel of POIN** (v4.2, older) — [Telkomsel FAQ 4.2](https://www.telkomsel.com/en/support/faq/about-mytelkomsel-42)

- AyoLari running tracker with challenges and prizes drove early downloads (Oct 2024) — [Bisnis.com](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20241029/101/1811651/fitur-ayolari-magnet-baru-mytelkomsel-tarik-pengguna)

## Marketing features: loyalty, cross-sell, personalization, referral

### Takeaway
MyTelkomsel is a full super app. It cross-sells IndiHome/Orbit, finance (Telkomsel Wallet by GoPay, QRIS), entertainment (MAXstream, Vidio, Langit Musik, Noice), travel, vouchers from 100+ brands, bills (BPJS, electricity), and Prestige benefits, all with AI personalization. by.U focuses on uTainment content, merchant vouchers (e.g., TikTok Shop), uCoin loyalty, and referral.

### Cited Findings
- Super app scope: health (running), travel (flights, check-in), bills (BPJS, PLN), hotels, vouchers from 100+ brands, goods from 70+ local brands, entertainment partners (INFIA, MAXstream, Langit Musik, Noice, SEA Today, Vidio), AI search. More than 46 million customers use the app — [Telkomsel press release, Jul 2024](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/mytelkomsel-hadir-sebagai-super-app-yang-memberikan-kemudahan-transaksi-dan)
- Finance menu with QRIS (9.2), "Siscamling" anti-scam protection (9.3), subscription management and gifting (9.4), Prestige benefits (8.x) — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/mytelkomsel/id651412430)
- May 2025: MyTelkomsel won "Telco to Ace Mobile App" at the Twimbit Telecom Awards 2025 for its AI use. Telkomsel claims 100M+ downloads and about 83M MAU — [Telkomsel news](https://www.telkomsel.com/about-us/news/telkomsel-raih-penghargaan-internasional-atas-pemanfaatan-ai-pada-mytelkomsel-di) (figures from the search summary)
- by.U: U-Tainment, TikTok Shop vouchers, top-up without the app, 10M+ active users in Q3 2025, and plans to grow into a lifestyle ecosystem (gaming, music, community) — [Selular.id, Oct 2025](https://selular.id/2025/10/by-u-tembus-10-juta-pengguna-kuasai-pasar-digital-operator/); [Bisnis.com, 19 Oct 2025](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20251019/101/1921548/byu-layani-10-juta-pelanggan-telkomsel-hingga-tri-berebut-pasar-gen-z)

## User sentiment and UX/business oddities

### Takeaway
Store ratings are high (MyTelkomsel 4.7 on iOS from 1.5M ratings; by.U 4.7 on iOS from 522 ratings). Studies and media, however, report recurring complaints about MyTelkomsel: slowness and crashes, login/OTP failures, confusing navigation after updates, pop-up offers, non-real-time quota display, and inconsistent or personalized prices. Most of these sources are student theses and older items. Recent independent tech-media coverage of app UX was scarce.

### Cited Findings
- MyTelkomsel iOS: 4.7/5 from 1.5M ratings, app size 382.8 MB, category Lifestyle. Positive 2025 reviews cite complete features, easy package buying, IndiHome bill payment, and speed. Older reviews (2019–2020) cite login/OTP failures when out of quota and dropped support for old iPhones — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/mytelkomsel/id651412430)
- Complaints in academic analyses of Play/App Store reviews: slow app, missing packages, confusing features, crashes, slow loading, confusing navigation, failed logins, non-real-time quota, frequent SMS and pop-up ads, non-transparent quotas, inconsistent promo prices, "different prices shown to different customers" — [Telkom Univ. sentiment study](https://repository.telkomuniversity.ac.id/pustaka/files/239491/bab1/analisis-sentimen-opini-publik-terkait-layanan-kuota-internet-aplikasi-mytelkomsel-melalui-ulasan-di-playstore-dan-appstore-dalam-bentuk-buku-karya-ilmiah.pdf); [Telkom Univ. management journal](https://openlibrarypublications.telkomuniversity.ac.id/index.php/management/article/download/29826/28125/58854); [IT Telkom Purwokerto](https://repository.ittelkom-pwt.ac.id/10596/4/BAB%20I.pdf); [UPN Jatim thesis](https://repository.upnjatim.ac.id/54010/1/BAB%201.pdf) (detail as summarized by search; the PDFs were not opened)
- Large outage Jan 2021: thousands of complaints, which Telkomsel attributed to system load — [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/teknologi/20210119175248-192-595793/netizen-riuh-mytelkomsel-eror)
- Telkomsel/IndiHome network outage in Jan 2026 (network, not app) — [Bisnis.com, 22 Jan 2026](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20260122/101/1946312/internet-telkomsel-dan-indihome-down-ini-cara-cek-jaringan-dan-lapor-ke-cs)
- by.U iOS reviews praise the cheap packages and stable signal. One asks for game-specific add-ons because data runs out quickly when gaming (2020) — [App Store by.U](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/by-u-id/id1483475992)

### Inferences (UX/business oddities)
- **Personalized and inconsistent pricing** helps conversion and margin but damages trust, because users compare prices and feel cheated. This is "hidden cheapest package" territory.
- **Super-app bloat** (382.8 MB, travel, health, running, mini-games) competes with the core job of checking quota and buying a package. It also explains the separate Lite/Basic app, which is a self-admitted clutter signal.
- **Pop-up top-up and promo pop-ups** (9.1 "pop-up top-up", real-time promos) increase sales but are a recurring irritant in reviews.
- **by.U lifetime SIM validity** removes a forced-recharge lever (a revenue trade-off) in exchange for trust and retention among youth.
- **The high-rating vs complaint gap** may come from in-app rating prompts. This is an inference only.

### Gaps
- Reddit and Kaskus threads were not reviewed (out of call budget).
- Recent (2025–2026) Play Store review text was not retrieved because the Play pages failed to load.
- No independent design case study (Medium/Behance) was verified. A Contra "MyTelkomsel App Redesign" concept exists but was not opened — [Contra](https://contra.com/p/QrBgveB6-my-telkomsel-app-redesign)
