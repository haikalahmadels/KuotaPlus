Everything about exact visual layout below is therefore either from store text/changelogs or marked as inference.

## 1. Home screen layout, navigation, where buy-package / top-up live

### Takeaway
Store listings confirm the functional blocks (balance + quota check, buy package/top-up, history, lifestyle/Digital Hub, rewards, search) and a user-pinnable "favorite features" section, but no source documents the exact home layout, card structure, or bottom-nav labels/count for either app in 2025-2026.

- myIM3 functional blocks listed in store description: check balance/data/calls/SMS, transaction & usage history, flash sales, IMPoin redemption, buy pulsa/packages/pay postpaid, "myIM3 Kiosk" (resell pulsa/packages to friends), "Ask Indira" virtual assistant, postpaid contract services, starter-pack purchase/activation, favorite package list, 4G coverage check, real-time notifications, PIN/PUK info, store info with online booking — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/id888610275?l=en)
- myIM3 v82.3.0 (28/08/2024): users can pin favorite features to the home page and the lifestyle page — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/id888610275?l=en)
- myIM3 has a separate "lifestyle page" (implied by the pin feature) and since Feb 2025 a "Digital Hub" section — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/id888610275?l=en); [Indotelko, 20 Feb 2025](https://www.indotelko.com/read/1740006083/aplikasi-myim3-dan-bima-hadirkan-digital-hub)
- bima+ (iOS, Hutchison 3 Indonesia, id1276444056): 4.6/5 from ~63k ratings; No. 5 Lifestyle; 218.6 MB; describes itself as "simpler, more exciting and more personal"; core features: check remaining quota and active packages, buy packages, multiple digital payments — [App Store bima+](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/bima/id1276444056?l=en)
- bima+ v5.3.0 (22/08/2024) added "Pin Section"; v4.3.9 (10/09/2023) added a "Lifestyle menu" and Member Get Member — [App Store bima+](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/bima/id1276444056?l=en)
- Both apps have a search field that since Aug 2025 is AI semantic search (see section 6) — [Selular.id, 22 Aug 2025](https://selular.id/2025/08/indosat-dan-google-cloud-rilis-fitur-pencarian-ai-di-myim3-dan-bima/)
- myIM3 v82.1.0 (17/05/2024) introduced guest mode; v82.2.0 (12/07/2024) social-account login for users without an IM3 number — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/id888610275?l=en)

### Inferences
- Both apps follow the same IOH platform pattern (near-identical changelog wording and release cadence, shared Digital Hub and AI search launches), suggesting a shared codebase/design system with brand skins. Evidence: matching "anti-spam protection, stronger network coverage" notes (myIM3 82.10-82.12, bima+ 5.9.0) and matching "personalized offers / data protection" notes in Feb 2025.
- "Pin to home" means home is modular (user-customizable shortcut grid), a pattern Kuota+ could copy cheaply.
- Guest mode + social login means the app is used as a marketing/acquisition channel for non-subscribers, not just self-care.

### Gaps
- Exact home order (pulsa vs kuota vs masa aktif first), card structure, banner carousel, bottom-nav item names and count: not found in any text source; needs manual check of Play Store screenshots or the app itself.
- myIM3 / bima+ Play Store ratings and download counts: Play page fetch failed.

## 7. User sentiment and illogical UX/business items

### Takeaway
Recurring complaints: slow loading, frequent package-offer pop-ups, inaccurate remaining-quota/validity info, self-logout, failed purchases/payments, crashes after updates. Praise is thin in fetched sources (bima+: "accurate, fast, reliable", 2022). Ratings remain decent (myIM3 4.3, bima+ 4.6 on iOS ID).

### Cited Findings
- Thesis analysing myIM3 Play Store reviews: long loading, frequent package-offer pop-ups, inaccurate remaining quota, failures; respondents report frequent self-logout, failed purchase, failed login — [IT Telkom Purwokerto, Bab I](https://repository.ittelkom-pwt.ac.id/8241/5/BAB%20I.pdf); recommendations to fix login errors and speed up loading — [Bab V](https://repository.ittelkom-pwt.ac.id/8241/6/BAB%20V.pdf)
- myIM3 iOS reviews: validity shown as 0 days (2019); unusable after iOS 17 (07/11/2023) — [App Store ID](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/id888610275?l=en); force close after updates, frequent logout (multiple storefronts) — [App Store CA](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/myim3/id888610275)
- bima+ iOS: paid via bank VA but quota never arrived, package expired, no compensation (13/12/2024); crash on launch on iPhone 6 (05/2024); praise "accurate, fast, reliable" (08/05/2022) — [App Store bima+](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/bima/id1276444056?l=en)
- Rollover package gives less quota than regular at same price (2026) — [Bisnis.com](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20260928/101/2007945/im3-indosat-punya-rollover-istimewa-harga-hampir-mirip-paket-freedom)

### Inferences
- Illogical points to avoid in Kuota+: (a) two separate apps for the same company with overlapping features (IM3 vs Tri) — dual maintenance, same features shipped twice; (b) pop-up offers interrupting self-care tasks; (c) stale quota/validity numbers undermining trust in the core metric; (d) failed-payment-with-money-taken with no in-app recovery path; (e) bloated app size from cross-sell.
- Recent changelogs are generic "performance and bug fixes" (bima+ 5.10-5.17, myIM3 82.13-82.18), giving users no visible reason to update.

### Gaps
- No recent (2025-2026) Indonesian media article or Reddit/Kaskus thread specifically about myIM3/bima+ complaints found; "kuota lokal vs utama" confusion for IOH not sourced.
